Technical Specifications

Detailed material and engineering specifications for our audiometric booths.

Engineering Details

Built to the Highest Standards

Every component is precision-selected to ensure maximum acoustic isolation, long-term durability, and safe operation.

Panel Thickness

100mm total wall & roof panel thickness for maximum sound attenuation.

Noise Reduction

Achieves <40 dB(A) inside from 60–65 dB(A) ambient as per OSHA norms.

Fire Resistant

All insulation materials are fire-resistant and water-repellent for long-term safety.

Modular Design

Fully modular panels allow easy assembly, disassembly and relocation at any time.
