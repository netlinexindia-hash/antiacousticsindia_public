Commercial Terms

1
Ex-Works Pricing: All prices are Ex-Works, Noida. Transportation charges to the project site are extra and will be quoted separately based on location.
2
Payment Schedule: 50% advance with order confirmation, 40% on dispatch from our facility, and 10% after installation and commissioning/testing.
3
GST: Goods and Services Tax (GST) at the prevailing rate of 18% is applicable extra on all quoted prices.

Installation: On-site installation and commissioning is included in the quoted price. Our team will handle the complete assembly at your project location.

Client Scope: Unloading of materials at site, scaffolding (if required for height work), and access ladders are in the client's scope and responsibility.
6
Utilities: Single-phase electrical supply for power tools during installation to be arranged and provided by the client at no extra cost.
7
Delivery Timeline: 2–3 weeks from the date of advance payment receipt and approval of drawings/specifications. Actual delivery subject to mutual agreement.
